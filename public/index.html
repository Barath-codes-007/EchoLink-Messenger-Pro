Temporary & permanent rooms

Burn-after-reading rooms for quick huddles, or permanent rooms with a room code anyone can rejoin, months later.

1:1 voice & video calls

WebRTC calls right inside a room — mute, camera toggle, screen share, and camera switching, no plugins.
